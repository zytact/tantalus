# Reset credits

Direct Codex fetches usage, reset credits, and its subscription before publishing a new Ready reading. Claude reads its banked resets from the same usage response. The Codex and Claude sections show Reset credits after their windows, so it is not a fixed numbered entry: a monthly-only Codex pool places it second. It is the last Codex entry, while Claude's Extra usage follows it. Opencode has no reset-credit field or section. A Codex account's row also shows `Subscription period ends <date>` when the reported end is in the future.

The entry is named `Reset credits`. Its headline is the number banked across the pool's accounts, leaving out an account whose count is unavailable. Under it each account has a column: its chip, its own count (`None` for zero, `Unavailable` when unread), and one `Expires <date>` line per credit. An account with credits but no detail rows shows `No credit details available.` Direct Codex accepts credit arrays under `credits`, `data`, or `items`, and prefers `available_count` for the headline. Hub Codex counts only available, unexpired `codex_rate_limits` credits with date-string expiries. A hub credit-read failure leaves usage Ready with credits unavailable.

## Preview proof

In Tantalus Preview on `--mock` `ready`, enable Opencode and Refresh: Codex reads 2 with two `Expires` lines, Claude reads 1 before Extra usage, and Opencode has no Reset credits. Then add the fixture hub per `proxy-hubs.md`, which switches direct Codex and Claude off, and confirm the hub's Codex and Claude read the same. Codex shows its subscription end. Switch to `monthly-only` and Refresh to see a headline of 0 and `None` for the Codex account. In real mode, enable Codex and Refresh. Capture the window after completion. Confirm Reset credits is last, its count matches the response, and each detail has an expiry or `No expiry reported`. On `hub-multiple` with the fixture hub, the Codex headline reads 4 over two columns of 2, and Claude reads 2 over two columns of 1.
