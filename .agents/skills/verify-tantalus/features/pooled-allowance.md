# Pooled allowance

The Allowance page has one section per provider that has an account, in Codex, Claude, Opencode order. A section holds every account of that provider, whether it is a direct sign-in or comes through a CLIProxyAPI hub, so two hubs that both carry Claude accounts make one Claude section. There is no section per hub. Opencode has no hub support, so its pool holds direct sign-ins only.

The heading shows the provider name and `1 account` or `<n> accounts pooled`. Under it, `list "<Provider> accounts"` has one row per account: a two-letter chip, the email blurred until its `Show email for <label>` button is clicked, the plan, the hub's label for a hub account, and the status word (`Live`, `Cached`, `Could not refresh`, `Not signed in`, `Loading`, `Blocked until reset`). The chip is the first letter of the email's local part and the first letter of its domain, so `codex@hub.test` reads `CH`, tinted with a colour of its own per email so two accounts sharing letters still differ. An account without an email is named by its label and its chip reads `#<n>`, its place in the pool. A Codex row adds `Subscription period ends <date>`, a row with a window start adds its `Started a window` line, and a failed or cached reading adds its notice.

Each window any account reports is one entry, in Short, Long, Fable, Monthly order. Its headline is the pool's remaining figure: the mean of what each account has left, over the accounts that report that window. One account's pool reads that account's own figure. The pace label compares the mean used with the mean elapsed share, over the accounts that have a reset time. Under the headline is one bar per account, at most three to a row. Each is `progressbar "<account label> <window> remaining"`, such as `Codex short window remaining` for a lone direct sign-in or `Fixture hub · Codex 1 short window remaining` for a hub account, with a pace marker and, when the account's pace calls for one, its dragon or tortoise. Creatures are drawn smaller in a pool of several accounts. The caption under a bar repeats the chip, then the remaining percentage and `↻ <countdown>`, read as `resets in <countdown>`, with the absolute reset time in its tooltip. A window nothing has been spent from reads `Not started`. A window whose reset has passed since it was read shows full and `Not started` until the next reading. An account without that window keeps its column as a dashed gap reading `No <window>`. In a pool of several accounts a line under the bars names the account that resets first and the share of the pool that hands back, such as `CH resets in 1h 0m, handing back 46%`.

After the windows come `Reset credits` (see `reset-credits.md`) and, for Claude, `Extra usage` (see `extra-usage.md`), each with a pooled headline and a column per account. Nothing on the page needs a click to expand.

Each switched-on hub keeps a section of its own under the providers, named by its label: its status (`Live`, `Loading`, `Cached`, `Could not refresh`, `Access refused`), the `Hub` management link on the machine that owns it, and its message when it has no supported accounts or could not be read. Its accounts are in the provider sections above.

`providerPools` and `poolWindows` in `src/shared/pool.ts` build the pools, `src/renderer/pool-section.tsx` draws a section, and `HubSection` in `src/renderer/main.tsx` draws a hub's row.

## Preview proof

Launch `--mock ready`, enable Opencode and Refresh. Confirm three sections, each reading `1 account`, each with one account row, and each headline equal to its only bar: Codex Short 60%, Claude Short 9%, Opencode Short 95%. Capture both schemes.

Run `mock-scenario.sh hub-multiple`, add the fixture hub per `proxy-hubs.md`, and Refresh. Direct Codex and Claude switch off, so the Codex and Claude sections now read `2 accounts pooled` with rows for `codex@hub.test` and `codex2@hub.test` (both chips `CH`, in different tints) and for `claude@hub.test` and `claude2@hub.test`, each row ending in `Fixture hub` and its status. Both accounts of a provider carry the same readings, so each headline equals each of its two bars: Codex Short 60%, Long 88%; Claude Short 9%, Long 45%, Fable 80%. Each entry has two progressbars, named `Fixture hub · Codex 1 short window remaining` and `Fixture hub · Codex 2 short window remaining`, and a `resets in` line under them. Opencode still reads `1 account`. Under the providers, `Fixture hub` shows `Live` and its `Hub` link. Capture the whole page in both schemes and confirm the chips, bars, captions and dashed gaps stay legible in each.

For a creature in a pool, keep the hub saved, run `seed-creatures.sh`, then `launch.sh --restart` and doctor (see `pace.md`). The Codex Short window must show a dragon on `Fixture hub · Codex 1`'s bar only, smaller than on a lone account's bar, and focusing it must open a tooltip that stays inside the window in both schemes.

For a headline that is a mean of different figures, use the offline suite: `vp test src/shared/pool.test.ts` covers the mean, the account counted once, a window that has reset, the next reset and the pooled pace.
