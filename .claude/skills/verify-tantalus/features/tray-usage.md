# Tray usage

Settings has a Usage in tray switch, off by default. Switched on, the tray icon shows one account's remaining percentage beside the mark, drawn in its provider's color: Codex blue, Claude orange, Opencode violet. An account without a 5-hour window, such as Codex Go or free, shows its monthly window instead. On Windows the number replaces the mark. The tooltip becomes `<product>` then `<account> <5h|30d> <percent> remaining` on a second line, where `<account>` is `<hub> · <Provider> <n>` for a hub account, never its email, and a pool inserts `of <n × 100>%` before `remaining`, where n counts accounts with a reading. Until the chosen account has a reading, or once it is no longer read, the tray keeps its plain icon and tooltip.

Below the switch, an Account picker lists every direct sign-in, led by `<Provider> · All <n> accounts` for a provider with several and naming each as `<Provider> · <email>` (`<Provider> <n>` without an email), then each hub's accounts in allowance order, as `<hub> · <Provider> · <email>` (`<hub> · <Provider> <n>` without an email). A hub with two or more accounts of one provider leads its block with `<hub> · <Provider> · All <n> accounts`, which adds up their remaining 5-hour allowance, so three accounts at 80%, 20% and 10% used read 190% of 300% remaining. The number can pass 100 and counts down toward 0. A window whose reset has passed since its last reading contributes a full 100%, and an account without a 5-hour reading is left out. Emails stay blurred in the closed picker and in the menu until a `Show emails` button beside the legend reveals them all (`Hide emails` blurs them again), or the email is revealed elsewhere on the page, with a Tray colors legend of the providers on offer. With no saved choice it shows the first account. The picker is a glass pop-up button (`combobox "Account"`) whose menu (`listbox "Account"`) checks the selected account. It opens on click or ArrowDown/ArrowUp; ArrowUp, ArrowDown, Home and End move the highlight; Enter or Space picks; Escape, Tab or a click outside closes without a change. A saved account that is no longer read stays selected as a disabled `Account no longer available` entry. With no provider or hub on, the row reads `Switch on a provider or add a hub to pick an account.` The choice is saved to `tray-usage.json` in the settings directory.

`src/renderer/tray-usage-settings.tsx`, `src/renderer/picker.tsx` and `src/renderer/account-name.tsx` render the row, `src/shared/tray-usage.ts` lists the accounts and picks the reading, and `trayUsageIcon` in `src/main/index.ts` draws the icon with `src/main/tray-icon.ts`.

## Preview proof

Launch with `--mock ready`, open Settings, and switch on Usage in tray. Snapshot `combobox "Account"` reading `Codex` and a legend of Codex and Claude. Scroll to the combobox and click it, then capture the open menu under `--scheme light` and `--scheme dark`. Each must show a blurred, tinted glass menu with legible rows and a check on Codex, never a white native list. Press ArrowDown and Enter; the combobox must read `Claude` and `tray-usage.json` in `mock-home/config/dev.arnab.tantalus.preview/` must hold `"source":"claude:home"`. A file saved by an older version with a bare provider id such as `"source":"claude"` still selects that provider's pool, or its only direct sign-in. Reopen, press Escape, and confirm nothing changed. Click the combobox, click `option "Codex"`, and confirm it saves. Run `launch.sh --restart` and doctor, then confirm the choice survived.

Write `{"enabled":true,"source":"hub:gone"}` to `tray-usage.json`, restart, and confirm the combobox reads `Account no longer available`, its menu lists that entry disabled and checked, and the highlight opens on the first real account. Add the fixture hub per `proxy-hubs.md` and confirm the menu adds `Fixture hub · Codex · codex@hub.test` and `Fixture hub · Claude · claude@hub.test`, with both emails blurred.

For pools, launch `--mock hub-multiple`, add the fixture hub, and confirm the menu leads with `Fixture hub · Codex · All 2 accounts` and `Fixture hub · Claude · All 2 accounts`. Pick a pool and check `tray-usage.json` holds `"source":"<hub id>:codex"`. Confirm the menu's emails stay blurred while highlighted, click `Show emails` and confirm they read clearly, click `Hide emails`, then click an email on the dashboard or in a Settings row and confirm the picker shows it too. On Windows a pool number wider than the 16 px square widens the icon, which the tray scales down.

The icon and tooltip need a tray host (see `tray-and-autorefresh.md`). The harness preview inherits the real session bus, so it registers `org.freedesktop.StatusNotifierItem-<pid>-1` there while its window sits on the isolated display. Read `ToolTip` on `/StatusNotifierItem` and confirm its second line names the chosen account, span, and percentage. Export the icon itself with `scripts/tray-icon.sh <dest.png> [preview-pid]`: it reads `IconThemePath` from `org.kde.StatusNotifierItem` on `/StatusNotifierItem` and copies the newest PNG there (Electron writes the icon into that directory and leaves `IconPixmap` unset). On Linux the icon is a single 16 px image with no 2x representation, since Chromium hands the panel its largest representation and GNOME's AppIndicator reserves a wide icon's full pixel width while drawing it at panel height: confirm the PNG is 16 px tall, its digits 10 px tall in the provider color, and its width the 16 px mark plus the 2 px gap plus the digits with nothing extra (currently 32 px wide for two digits, 40 for three). macOS keeps 18/36 and Windows a 16/32 square, both with 1x and 2x representations.

For the remaining display, `ready` must show direct Codex 60, Claude 9, and Opencode 95 beside the icon, with matching percentages labeled `remaining` in the tooltip and menu. `hub-multiple` must show Codex 120% of 200% remaining and Claude 18% of 200% remaining for their pools; an individual account still reads 60% or 9%. On `monthly-only`, direct and hub Codex fall back to 30d at 42% remaining. On `idle`, direct and hub Codex and Claude read 100% remaining; on `blocked`, their 5h rows and Opencode read 0% remaining with empty bars. On `no-windows`, the selected account has no tray number and the tooltip returns to the product name.
