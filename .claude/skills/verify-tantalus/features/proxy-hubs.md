# Proxy hubs

Settings lists CLIProxyAPI hubs under `Proxy hubs`. `Add hub` opens a form with `Hub URL`, `Management key`, and an optional `Label`. The main process reads a new hub before saving it, so a URL or key that does not work is never saved and the form shows the cause. The saved key stays in the main process, so an edit starts with the key blank. A blank key keeps the old one only on the same URL origin; a different origin requires re-entry. A new URL or key is read again before saving; a new label alone is not. Show and Hide management key control only the value being typed.

Each hub row has a `<label> proxy hub` switch, `Edit <label>`, and `Remove`. A switched-on hub gets its own allowance section after the direct providers, with one block per pooled Codex or Claude account showing provider and plan, and its own tray block. Each account's email is blurred until its button is clicked; a hidden one reads `Show email for <hub> · <Provider>`. Revealing an email shows it everywhere the page lists that account, in Settings too, until the page reloads. Settings names hub accounts `<hub> · <Provider> · <email>`. An account the hub reports no email for is `<hub> · <Provider> <n>`, where `<n>` counts that provider's accounts without an email in the hub. Opencode hub accounts are unsupported. A saved hub that contains Codex or Claude switches off the matching direct provider and shows a toast. Trying to enable that direct provider shows a toast asking you to remove the hub or remove that provider from it first. A saved hub keeps blocking its providers while switched off. Remove the hub, or remove the provider from it and refresh its roster, to release the direct provider. The underlined Hub link with an external-link arrow on the dashboard opens `/management.html` at the saved hub origin in the browser. Remote devices do not show it. A hub that refuses its key stops being read until it is switched off and on again or successfully edited with a working URL/key. Accounts share their usual pace with other accounts on the same provider, reported tier, and window length, including direct sign-ins. Current pace and quotas stay separate.

## Preview proof

On `--mock ready`, open Settings, click `Add hub`, then `drive.ts fill textbox "Hub URL" http://127.0.0.1:<fixture port>` from `fixture.port` in the run directory, `fill textbox "Management key" fixture-management`, and `fill textbox "Label Optional" "Fixture hub"`. Click the form's `Add hub`. Settings must list `Fixture hub` with its switch on, and Allowance must show a Codex Pro and a Claude Max 5x account. Confirm both direct switches turn off with a toast, and trying either one again leaves it off with a removal message. Confirm the dashboard hub URL points at the fixture host and `/management.html`. Switch the hub off and confirm the direct providers stay blocked, then switch it back on. Edit the label with the key blank and confirm it saves without a hub read. Try a different URL origin with the key blank and confirm the form asks for the key again. Restore the label, then try adding another hub with a wrong fixture key; its error must show the refusal and it must not be saved. Cancel the form, click `Remove`, and confirm the direct providers can be enabled before teardown.
